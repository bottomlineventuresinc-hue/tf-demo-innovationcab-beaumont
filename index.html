What we will not do

  • Hang cabinets before the walls and floor are checked for level and plumb.
  • Quote cabinet installation from photos alone.
  • Hide material costs inside a vague line item.
  • Start before the layout, including what comes out first, is agreed.
  • Leave packaging, offcuts, or dust behind.
